Support

Frequently Asked Questions

What warranty and service support is included?

KENT purifiers come with a 1-Year Warranty plus 3-Year Free Service AMC support covering electrical components and technician visits pan-India.

How does the Free Home Demonstration work?

A certified technician visits your home with a demo unit, tests your municipal or borewell tap water purity on the spot, and shows how the system works without any purchase obligation.

What is Mineral RO™ and how does it help?

Traditional RO purifiers strip all minerals making water acidic. KENT Mineral RO has a patented TDS controller that retains essential natural minerals like calcium and magnesium.

Can it handle high-TDS borewell and tanker water?

Yes. KENT RO purifiers are engineered for raw water TDS up to 2000 ppm, automatically neutralizing heavy dissolved salts, pesticides, fluorides, and contaminants.

How do I know when the filter needs replacement?

The unit features a built-in filter change alarm that alerts you with an audible beep. In addition, KENT service centers track your installation date and reach out to schedule visits.
